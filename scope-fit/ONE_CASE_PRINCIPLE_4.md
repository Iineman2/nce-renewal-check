# Feature 1.2 Principle 4: uncertainty must remain visible

Expected behavior: one uniquely matching source row is a candidate. It becomes a confirmed intended case only after an explicit source attestation. Missing usable source account/customer identity or exact valid renewal date remains unresolved and cannot be overridden by questionnaire answers, source-link matches, attestation, eligibility claims or default selection.

The existing private canonicalCaseSubject remains the owner of selection, structured uncertainty codes, readiness, count, evidence and bounded ambiguity previews. selectCaseSubject returns candidate/unresolved, never confirmed. assertCaseSubject independently rebuilds that projection. assertCaseSelectionReview independently captures and rebuilds the complete private confirmation review before rendering or gating; inconsistent state, evidence, readiness or accessor projections fail closed. Duplicate identical or differing rows, including cross-account matches, produce no chosen subject; previews show the first 20 logical records with total count and explicit truncation. They are evidence summaries, not choices. Source repair or a corrected exact ID followed by rerun is required.

confirmCaseSelection rejects incomplete or ambiguous evidence. Its frozen, ephemeral receipt has private WeakMap ownership binding the exact supplied decoded CSV text and exact selector input. Serialized, cloned, forged or stale receipts confer no confirmation. reviewCaseSelection independently rebuilds source readiness and exposes confirmed only for the matching private receipt. This avoids hashing assumptions and copying a potentially 2MB source into a rendered projection. Receipts intentionally do not survive reload or persistence. App control snapshots and independent subject render serial invalidate detached callbacks, edits, replacement, cancel, clear, reset, policy/calendar changes and late reads.

The Current case card preserves original evidence when identity is incomplete or the later HaloPSA read fails. An independent keyboard-accessible source confirmation works without a usable HaloPSA file. Existing link attestation explicitly confirms the complete intended case and billing link together; adjacent text states account/customer/subscription/renewal scope. No automatic confirmation or mandatory redundant attestation is introduced. Selection uncertainty blocks comparison progression, conflict choices and link attestation; the comparison engine remains a separate claims classifier. Missing namespace is not classified as outside scope.

Identifiers preserve the existing trim-only, case/Unicode-sensitive contract. Account unknown vocabulary is unusable; customer/subscription identifiers are opaque syntactically valid IDs, so an actual identifier spelled "unknown" is not silently rewritten. Names, products, seats, filename, ordinal, and questionnaire date cannot complete identity. Confirmation is self-attested and unauthenticated; it establishes no eligibility, signed coverage or financial authority.

All previous model/browser/syntax scripts and prior audit inventories remain required under output/one-case-principle-4/qc.py; negative evidence mutations must be rejected. Historical receipts are preserved.
