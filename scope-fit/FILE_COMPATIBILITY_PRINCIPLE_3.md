# Feature 1.3 Principle 3: enforceable resource bounds

Expected behavior defined 4 October 2026 and clarified after the 5 October adversarial audit. Original measured evidence remains in output/file-compatibility-principle-3/REPORT.md. The repair and new qualification are tracked separately in output/file-compatibility-principle-3-closure/REPORT.md; this contract alone is not a passing result.

An admitted CSV is processed under all current FILE_SUPPORT resource limits simultaneously. Limits measure observed bytes, decoded units, every lexical CSV source record/cell and lexical material, including blank/skipped records and ignored quoted-field whitespace. No success may describe a silently retained prefix as a complete file.

The browser uses dedicated module Workers for reading, hashing, decoding, scanning and profile checks. Completion transport resource-csv-v3 carries original bytes and bounded scalar text/metadata; its scan field must be null. A separate privately issued File.text observation is capped at the original admitted byte count and 2,000,000 UTF-16 units. It is null only when original-byte encoding prevents decoding. The receiver rejects transported scan graphs and reconstructs canonical lexical evidence through finite steps of the same sole lexer, then independently compares the observation to byte-owned decoded text with exactly one initial decoded BOM omitted and verifies original-byte custody and canonical profile diagnostics. A genuine disagreement preserves original inspection while blocking normalized progression; observed text never becomes parsed facts or case authority. Worker diagnostic prose must match the independently derived observation or profile outcome. This is bounded derivative verification, never a synchronous acquisition fallback. At most two jobs run, with no implicit queue and at most 4,000,000 admitted input bytes in flight. Each job has a monotonic deadline no greater than 30,000ms from admission through custody publication. Cancel, replacement, removal, relevant edits, reset, restoration and terminal failure terminate affected workers, settle each job once, clear handlers/timers/reservations and fence late messages. If native termination fails, admission halts and the affected reservation is quarantined until reload; it is never released as though work had stopped. Missing Worker capability never activates a synchronous browser fallback.

Cancellation acknowledgement is measured within 250ms and selection acknowledgement within 100ms on the qualified local reference run. Deadlines are enforced on the next runnable main event after the monotonic threshold and rechecked on messages and adoption; a suspended browser or OS cannot provide a wall-clock scheduling guarantee. A busy worker must actually be terminated, not merely leave a canceled label while its code continues.

Original evidence retains its existing byte-custody owner and exact role/File occurrence. Only privately issued current-job packets can be adopted. Packet identity, source/policy/operation, observed bytes, integrity and finite structure are checked. A canceled or timed-out job cannot publish custody or derived authority. A readable over-limit profile remains an explicit input-repair result, never compatible success; retained partial lexical evidence is labeled incomplete. Existing independent readable sources can remain inspectable through the appropriate lifecycle.

Repeated canonical reads reuse bounded immutable current-source scans and parsed rows. The cache holds at most two role/profile/policy entries for current texts and is released with custody or evicted under its fixed capacity. It cannot grow with history or replace the canonical lexical/profile/case interpreters.

Original previews show at most five records and at most 64 cells per source page; wide sources reduce page length. All admitted material remains available through paging and exact original recovery. Owned view, aggregate text, global DOM, ancestor-depth and cover-candidate caps are checked before cloning, serialization and costly style/range validation. Exceeding a presentation cap stops that presentation and its dependent actions rather than approving an uninspected view.

All applicable limits are published by FILE_SUPPORT, with their units and a versioned resource policy. In-flight input bytes, retained original bytes, transport/object counts and actual resident memory are distinct quantities. Defensive/native copies and derivatives add overhead. Qualification establishes finite algorithm and output bounds, not a resident-memory ceiling or physical erasure guarantee.

New archive/document/OCR readers remain unavailable. Before enabling any, its actual expansion, hidden/container structure, pixels and separate task deadline must attach to this contract and pass its own gates. CSV support supplies no evidence for decompression or OCR qualification.

Required quality gates include every numeric boundary at N-1/N/N+1 and intersecting limits; busy and stalled worker termination; abort-before-start and cancel/message/deadline races; malformed/foreign/late packets; capacity and retry; current-source cache lifecycle; paging/DOM caps; exact bytes/coordinates/canonical decisions; actual reference responsiveness; full regressions; preserved historical packs; source-bound receipts and cause-specific rejection controls; independent reviews and final verification.

Closure requirements: exact plain-data field inventories apply to every packet envelope, scan, record, cell, terminator location, empty run/first record and failure. Schema/type/count rejection precedes copying unknown payload fields. Actual UTF-8/UTF-16 byte classification owns the encoding diagnostic; verified decoded text cannot carry an encoding-failure label. Setup checks retirement after every external registration or construction step. Pending termination cancellation is consumed before packet validation. A failed optional error-event suppression method cannot delay settlement. Independent cleanup steps are all attempted even when one fails.

Packet data is built with exact own property definitions in compact temporary objects, then given a checked null prototype and frozen before return. The temporary objects remain unpublished. Inherited setters, getters, reserved property names and inherited thenables cannot supply copied data or authority. A throwing or ineffective prototype cutover fails closed. Private custody/packet handles retain their existing owners. A measured reduction in retained packet allocation is not a physical peak-memory limit or proof of a responsiveness fix.

Qualification must preserve timing failures as failures. The fifth closure capture failed its 100ms mixed-input/DOM heartbeat, and unchanged-source diagnostics reproduced intermittent gaps. On 6 October, concurrent maximum-size jobs reproduced current failures; two native traces identified back-to-back scan-graph deserialization occupying 137.423 and 121.024ms of the main thread before bounded processing could yield. The graph-free completion transport addresses that demonstrated mechanism. Earlier untraced pauses, including a 553.7ms concurrent gap, are not individually attributed by those traces. Current resource-csv-v3 repair evidence is recorded in `../output/playwright/resource-timing-20261006/REPORT.md`: 66/66 strict native stress cases (79.3ms worst against100ms), 599/599 model tests, 452/452 browser groups,29 automated accessibility scans,105 syntax checks and58 exact saved-original artifacts. Final local acceptance is PASS_WITHIN_SUPPORTED_LOCAL_CONTRACT; historical sealed files remain unchanged. A passing run never certifies every native/browser/OS scheduling scenario.

Canonical row preparation also uses a sole finite generator, constructing at most 256 row cells per step before the existing count/time-bounded runner yields. It checks current-job retirement before publishing a complete row cache and rolls back newly published rows if retirement occurs at the final boundary. Supplied preparation material has explicit header/key/cell/record/text caps before trimming, construction or diagnostic joining; a foreign scan cannot register lexical text, original custody or case authority. DOM ownership/depth accounting retains at most 64 ancestor entries and performs one fresh budget walk before each independent retained-view snapshot, after any source refresh. No budget result is cached across mutations.

The pending Cancel control has its own finite presentation, visibility, contrast, pointer and cover checks. Ordinary native interaction and targeted presentation faults are separately qualified. Timings measure the declared acknowledgement event rather than unrelated browser automation or replacement-file work. Acquisition deadlines do not imply an end-to-end workflow deadline.

Proof closure requires exact typed receipt outcomes and cleanup, actual reached phases, finite meaningful timing bounds, coherent complete model results and exactly one record per required independent review. The complete historical-output baseline is separately pinned by identity and normalized path inventory and included in source capture and sealing. Seal schema and UTC ordering are consumed. Post-seal verification replays complete-final negative cases against actual reviews and artifacts without rebinding or changing sealed leaves.

Held-job accounting is independently bound to the reached phase and trigger, including reserved original bytes and the exact current cache inventory. A completed cancellation cannot retain orphan bytes; pending native custody must retain its own reservation; a fresh replacement can be pending or published only with the matching job/byte/cache tuple. Whole-state alternatives are declared by the lifetime owner, never inferred from the receipt being verified.

Development clarification: cancel/deadline rejects and fences the job promptly and actually terminates its Worker. If an independent native WebCrypto custody check is already awaiting the browser platform, its two-job/input reservation remains held until that check settles and cleanup finishes. WebCrypto does not expose task cancellation. This bounds outstanding verification instead of releasing known work as though it had completed; physical platform crypto activity, native allocation erasure and OS scheduling are not certified. No canceled verification may acquire custody or publish a result.

Main packet verification uses the same finite-data capture owner as Worker production. Generator steps are bounded to 1,024 visited packet values, 256 schema/semantic fields, or a nominal 16,384-byte/text-unit walk. Completing a scalar at that boundary permits at most 16,387 UTF-8 bytes or 16,385 UTF-16 units in a scalar-validation step. Each round-trip encoder input is at most 16,384 scalar-aligned UTF-16 units, with at most 49,152 encoded bytes compared in that step. Lexical replay independently yields after 256 constructed cells or 16,384 UTF-16 units, so a short run of dense blank fields cannot create thousands of objects in one step. The async runner checks retirement before and after every finite step, yields the first pending step immediately, and thereafter yields after at most 16 finite steps or at the first between-step clock check observing at least four milliseconds since the batch started. A finite step can overrun that scheduling threshold; it is not a native preemption deadline. These count and time bounds work together; paying a native timer quantum for every tiny step must not consume the 30-second deadline. Canonical byte offsets, omitted-record replay and failure-frontier replay reuse the sole lexical owner. Custody byte comparison, Unicode validation and round-trip encoding preserve the same original-byte checks in bounded chunks. Profile publication has a separate task boundary and rechecks retirement before cache insertion. Its reservation remains held until cleanup; interruption cannot publish a partial packet or custody. These verification operations do not activate a synchronous browser acquisition fallback.

Selected native-file metadata is checked before Worker admission: filename <=16,384 UTF-16 units and media-type label <=1,024 units. Longer metadata produces owned input repair with no Worker, while the selected original remains untouched. This bounds metadata copying/display escaping; extensions and MIME labels still do not establish support or authenticity.

A viewport with width or height no greater than one CSS pixel is unobservable. Automatic retention checks preserve exact semantic inventories, file occurrence, custody and presentation budgets while deferring geometry. The common viewport predicate governs controls, active Evidence, File guide and pending read notices. Direct original recovery and active-view publication retain their strict observable-screen check; workflow clicks/submission and discovery admission cannot grant work or authority through the unobservable screen. Edits still invalidate and cancel pending work, and Cancel remains unconditional. Returning native metrics rechecks readability. This is a finite local state check, not acceptance of operating-system scheduling suspension.
