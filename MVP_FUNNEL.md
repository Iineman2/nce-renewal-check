# The first-customer funnel: trust-led, case-led, paid

**Status:** Historical founder-led funnel proposal. The current product-led funnel is in [SELF_SERVE_MVP.md](SELF_SERVE_MVP.md).

Decision date: 2026-09-29. This is the recommended first-customer funnel for a solo founder in Mumbai selling the [deduction-resolution MVP](MVP_SCOPE.md) into the U.S. market. It refines the [GTM plan](MVP_GTM_PLAN.md). It is a testable operating design, not a proven conversion model. No prospect has been contacted or customer case processed in this workspace.

## One funnel, two entry paths

**Primary entry path: a relevant U.S. practitioner introduction.** Identify a small number of independent retail-deduction, EDI, or order-to-cash advisers who already serve the target supplier. Ask for a process discussion and, only where they see a real fit, an introduction to the supplier's Finance/AR owner. Do not offer an untested reseller program, presume a referral, or hand over customer data without permission.

**Fallback and learning path: researched founder outreach.** Build a small account list from public evidence that a supplier sells to major U.S. retailers and has a deductions/AR owner. Contact one appropriate person at a time with a question about cases left open after the incumbent process. A public signal qualifies *who to ask*, never proves that the company has a residual queue.

Both paths converge on the same case-led qualification and paid offer. The founder owns discovery and delivery. A community such as [RVCF](https://www.rvcf.com/) shows that retailers, suppliers, and service providers discuss deductions and supply-chain issues; participation or introductions there are possibilities to investigate, not assumed distribution. Prioritize no-cost or low-cost remote access before any paid membership or event spend.

## The offer and the first message

**One-sentence offer:** “Give me a small set of material retailer deductions your existing process could not close; I will return source-backed decisions and customer-approved next-action packets, then track the financial result.” The pilot is a resolution sprint. [SPS offers a free business audit](https://www.spscommerce.com/products/revenue-recovery/), so a generic free audit would obscure this offer and would not test willingness to pay.

**Introduction request to a practitioner:** “I am studying the hard deduction cases that remain after the usual recovery platform or BPO workflow. If you see suppliers with a real unresolved queue, I would value 20 minutes to understand where the process stops. I have a bounded, read-only paid resolution sprint in mind and can show a synthetic case file. I will not contact or use client data without their permission.” This is proposed copy, not a message sent.

**Direct note to a supplier leader:** “When a retailer deduction is denied or stalls after your current process has run, who reconstructs the case and follows the money to final closure? I am testing a narrow paid sprint for that residual queue, starting with one retailer. Could we discuss one recent example? No files needed for a first call.” This is proposed copy, not a message sent.

On the first call, ask *what happened last time* rather than “would you use AI?” If the incumbent can close the example at an acceptable cost, mark it no fit. Do not solve real cases free in discovery. A redacted example may be used only with the supplier's permission and agreed handling.

## Founder workload and trust controls

- Work in small account batches; the initial 20–30 researched accounts in the GTM plan are a founder-time budget, not a conversion forecast. Keep one active deep-work pilot at a time and reserve bounded time for older open outcomes.
- Schedule U.S. Eastern/Central calls in predictable evening-IST blocks. Use written agendas, asynchronous evidence requests, and weekly case summaries so delivery does not require all-day U.S. coverage.
- Keep the customer's retailer credentials and external submissions with the customer initially. State exactly where uploaded data sits, who can access it, whether model providers process it, and when it is deleted. Agree on these details before receiving files.
- Use one synthetic case-file preview and one plain-language security note to demonstrate method. Do not invent recovered-dollar claims, U.S. presence, certifications, or customers.
- Confirm the buyer can onboard and pay an India-based vendor before prolonged solution work. A 30–45-day pilot clock starts only after contract and access; procurement duration is measured separately.
- The first case cap is a planning assumption, not a production promise. Price and scope from a realistic hours budget; stop accepting deep-work cases at the contracted cap.

## Instrument the funnel, not just the pilot

For every account, record source and fit evidence, introduction/direct path, contact, reply, meeting, real residual case shown, sponsor identified, approximate cohort value, data/access result, India-vendor objection, proposal, paid/not paid, and reason lost. Keep sales hours separate from delivery hours.

Diagnose the *stage of failure* before changing the product:

- Few conversations from high-fit accounts: message, credibility, or entry-channel problem.
- Conversations but no real post-incumbent cases: ICP/wedge problem.
- Cases exist but no sponsor, evidence permission, or India-vendor route: distribution/access problem for this founder.
- Qualified sponsors but no paid SOW: price, urgency, trust, or offer problem; collect their stated reason rather than assuming one.
- Paid pilots but no actions, verified outcomes, positive fixed-fee contribution, or repeat purchase: product/value or delivery-economics problem.

Do not scale outbound, buy ads, or build broad integrations to compensate for an unproven stage. A successful first funnel means **a paid, deliverable case cohort and a reasoned second-purchase decision**, not a large email list or demo count.
