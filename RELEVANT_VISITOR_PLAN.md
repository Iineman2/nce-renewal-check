# Acquiring the first relevant visitors

Plan date: 2026-09-29. This expands the first step of the [product-led MVP funnel](SELF_SERVE_MVP.md) for a solo founder in Mumbai. It is a proposed acquisition system, not evidence of traffic or demand. No website, outreach, partner introduction, or analytics implementation has been verified in this workspace.

## Define the visitor before choosing a channel

A **relevant discovery visitor** is an AR/deductions or Finance user at a supplier with a deduction that remains unresolved after its current process stalled, and who can discuss or sponsor a software evaluation. That problem and user were already selected in the MVP. A **relevant product visitor** additionally has a case within the first release's explicitly supported workflow. A visit from an arbitrary CPG employee, consultant, student, or supplier with only routine claims is not a qualified visit.

Before someone answers the in-product scope check, a targeted click is only a **possible fit**. Count confirmed fit separately. Do not infer an unresolved queue from company size, retailer logos, or job titles.

## Phase 0: use the decided problem to select the launch workflow

The MVP problem, user, and desired economic outcome are decided. The exact exception workflow the first release can analyze unaided has not been selected. Interview or observe a small number of permitted examples to choose a retailer/rule path, deduction reason and state, required documents, and next action. These are discovery conversations, not claimed product visits or free case-resolution work. Record why a candidate workflow is recurring, material, evidence-addressable, and narrow enough for unaided software guidance.

We can write an honest **problem-level acquisition message now**: “For retailer deductions your current process could not close, see how a software-guided case separates evidence, missing blockers, next action, and actual payment.” A public page can show a **synthetic** case and invite discovery or early access. It must not claim that visitors can upload any retailer deduction or that recovery is proven. After the launch workflow is chosen and tested, add a case-specific message and a live-case CTA. Example *format*, not a chosen claim: “Your [retailer] [deduction type] was denied. See what evidence and next action the case still needs.”

## The minimum destination

Build one page that a buyer can understand without a call:

1. A headline naming the decided post-incumbent problem and user; once the first workflow is supported, identify that exact scope prominently.
2. A short interactive synthetic case showing original claim, source-linked facts, conflict, missing item, recommended next step, customer approval, and a separate approved-versus-paid state.
3. An eligibility check: retailer, deduction reason/state, whether an existing process already attempted it, available documents, and user role. Unsupported cases get a clear explanation and an optional request to be notified of future coverage.
4. One primary action: **Try the example** before real data; when the live product and its controls are ready, **Start a supported case**. Keep an optional contact path for company approval or questions.
5. A trust page stating who operates the company, Mumbai location, supported scope, data location and processing, founder/support access, retention/deletion, and the limits of the product's recommendation. Publish only controls that actually exist.
6. A short explanation of payment timing and price once decided. Do not advertise a price or free real-case analysis that has not been approved as an experiment.

The page is a distribution destination and a qualification surface. It is not a broad “AI deduction management” website. A general document-upload demo would be hard to distinguish from [UpClear's current self-service document and packet offer](https://upclear.com/bluedeductions-express/).

## First acquisition loop: founder-curated, account-based

**Primary channel for the first iteration:** manually identify a small set of U.S. suppliers that plausibly have the decided post-incumbent problem, then invite the right AR/Finance user to the synthetic example. Once the launch workflow is supported, narrow outreach to accounts that plausibly encounter it. This uses founder research time rather than ad spend. Public signals can include direct retailer-supplier relationships, a deductions/O2C role, a relevant retailer support footprint, and the buyer's own description of exception work. Treat signals as reasons to ask, not proof of pain.

For each account, record the source of the fit signal, likely user and economic owner, relevant workflow, contact route, date, response, landing link used, and next step. Send a tailored note about the *case state* and the sample, not a generic recovery percentage or a request to upload files to an unknown vendor. Ask permission before using an introduction or sharing anyone's data. No outreach is authorized by this document alone.

**Example note after the workflow is chosen:** “I built a short interactive example for [specific exception] that remains open after the normal deduction process. It shows how the evidence, missing blocker, next action, and eventual payment are kept separate. If this is a case your team sees, would the example be useful? It uses synthetic data and requires no files.” This is proposed copy, not a sent message.

For commercial email into the U.S., check the [FTC's CAN-SPAM guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) before sending. Its requirements cover business-to-business commercial messages as well as bulk campaigns; use truthful sender and subject details, a valid postal address, and a working opt-out process. Do not treat personalization as an exemption.

## Secondary acquisition loops, after the primary one teaches us

- **Practitioner introductions:** ask a small number of relevant U.S. deduction, EDI, or O2C advisers whether the exact case occurs in their clients. An introduction can transfer trust, but no adviser relationship or referral volume has been established. The destination remains the product example, not a founder-delivered service.
- **One useful case-specific guide:** publish a researched checklist for the selected workflow with current source links and a concrete example. Use the words a user would search when that case stalls. [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) recommends useful, original, people-first content rather than pages made mainly to capture rankings. Expect search traffic to take time; do not depend on it for the first customers.
- **Relevant supplier communities:** participate where permitted with genuinely useful answers and a clear affiliation. Community presence is a way to learn and earn trust, not a guaranteed traffic source. No paid membership or event spend is justified before access and value are demonstrated.

Do not begin with paid ads, broad SEO content, bulk email, or a general launch on consumer startup sites. They can produce traffic without the supported case or buyer authority and would obscure whether this wedge works.

## What to build to operate the loop

| Asset or system | Minimum requirement |
|---|---|
| Product example | Interactive, synthetic, source-linked case with no customer data required. |
| Website and identity | A clear domain, truthful founder/company identity, supported-scope page, contact route, and data-handling explanation. |
| Account worksheet | A small spreadsheet or CRM with fit evidence, role, outreach path, response, and qualification result. No paid CRM is required to start. |
| Link attribution | Distinct campaign links or referral codes for direct outreach, practitioner introductions, and search; no sensitive case data in URLs or analytics. |
| Product events | Record page visit, example start/completion, eligibility result, account creation, case import, evidence-backed next step, payment, and repeat case. Keep aggregate measurement separate from confidential documents. |
| Outreach process | Small, researched batches, manual follow-up, accurate records of opt-outs, and weekly review of founder hours spent. |

## How to judge this stage

Review the funnel by **qualified progression**, not total page views:

Before live-case support: `approached relevant accounts → visits to the problem/example page → example completions → visitors confirming a real residual case → discovery or early-access interest`.

After live-case support: `visits → example completions → confirmed-fit eligibility checks → account creations → first supported case → paid use`.

Track numbers and reasons at each step, plus founder hours and any channel cost. If verified-fit people do not visit, revise targeting, the contact route, or the initial message. If they visit but do not try the example, revise the page and promise. If they finish the example but fail eligibility, the chosen workflow or targeting is wrong. If eligible users will not create an account or upload a real case, investigate trust, scope, permissions, and value before buying more traffic. Do not infer statistical conversion rates from a tiny first batch; use it to locate the failing step.

The first prelaunch success is an identifiable target user confirming a real post-incumbent exception and engaging with the example. After launch, success requires a user with a supported exception to start a case under permitted terms without a founder-led audit. Paid repeat use remains the later demand test.

## Unverified dependencies

The chosen workflow, qualified-account list, deliverable demo, buyer reachability, introduction access, page performance, data controls, and conversion rates are all unverified. An acquisition plan cannot compensate for a case workflow the software cannot actually support.
