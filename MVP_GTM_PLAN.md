# Go-to-market plan for the deduction-resolution MVP

**Status:** Historical founder-delivered pilot proposal. Its acquisition and delivery model is superseded by [SELF_SERVE_MVP.md](SELF_SERVE_MVP.md). Retained for the prior reasoning and economic constraints.

Plan date: 2026-09-29. Revised for a solo founder working from Mumbai. This is an execution hypothesis for the [MVP scope](MVP_SCOPE.md) and [behavior contract](MVP_BEHAVIOR.md), not evidence that demand or pricing has been validated. No outreach, contract, or customer delivery has occurred in this workspace.

## Founder-location constraint and market choice

The research supports a **U.S. retailer-deduction hypothesis**. Living in Mumbai does not make an India-first version of the same product validated: Indian retailer terms, dispute routes, payment practices, buying authority, and pricing would need fresh case-level research. Keep the first test U.S.-focused unless access to a real Indian supplier cohort provides a stronger paid path. If remote access to U.S. buyers and approved financial evidence proves impractical, treat that as a GTM failure of this wedge for this founder rather than quietly substituting a different market.

Operate remotely and disclose the founder's location. Prioritize U.S. Eastern/Central suppliers so short scheduled evening-IST calls overlap their business day; make evidence requests, case reviews, and status reports asynchronous. Budget regular overlap hours and avoid a delivery model that requires being available throughout a U.S. workday. Do not plan travel, a local sales hire, broad events, or paid advertising before the first repeatable paid result.

For one founder, **one active deep-work pilot at a time** is the initial capacity rule; reserve a bounded follow-through allowance for older cases still awaiting retailer or finance outcomes. Import the whole eligible cohort for the denominator, but contract only a small fixed subset for deep resolution work—an initial planning cap of up to 10 cases and 30–40 total delivery hours, to be reset after measuring the first cases. Case complexity can make even that cap too high. This replaces the earlier research example of 50–100 deep-work cases; that example has not been proven deliverable by one person in the proposed hours.

## Positioning and first customer

**Positioning:** “We work the retailer deductions your existing team, platform, or BPO has already identified but has not closed. For a bounded set of material exceptions, we reconstruct the evidence, tell you which cases are worth pursuing, prepare the approved next action, and track the financial result.” The differentiation must be demonstrated on post-incumbent cases, not asserted from generic deduction volume.

**Initial customer:** a U.S. consumer-products manufacturer selling directly to major retailers, with an existing deduction process, a dedicated AR/order-to-cash owner, and a measurable queue of unresolved or denied exceptions. Prefer a supplier large enough to have material cases but small enough that a Finance sponsor can purchase a narrow read-only service without an enterprise-wide transformation project. The sponsor must be comfortable with remote delivery from India under agreed data terms. Select one retailer, entity, and reason family from its real queue rather than guessing in advance.

**Buyer and operators:** start with the Controller, VP Finance, or AR/O2C leader who owns the dollars and can sponsor a pilot. The deductions manager is the day-to-day champion. Finance/cash application verifies the result; Logistics or Commercial supplies evidence and approves sensitive routes; Procurement and IT handle the narrow data/SOW path. An analyst's enthusiasm without an economic sponsor and permitted data access is not a qualified opportunity.

## Offer: a paid hard-case resolution sprint

Sell a 30–45-day, fixed-scope paid pilot **after contracting and access are complete**; a U.S. buyer's vendor-onboarding time is a separate, unproven sales-cycle risk. The customer provides a read-only unresolved-case export and approved supporting documents. The contract fixes the cohort, retailer/entity/reason family, case-selection rule, maximum deep-work cases and delivery hours, required contacts, approval authority, data handling, follow-through period for open outcomes, and a production decision date. Within ten business days after required records arrive, deliver evidence-backed case files with a decision, missing blocker, exact next action, owner, and deadline. The customer approves consequential external actions. Final reporting separates submitted, retailer-approved, finance-verified recovered, correctly accepted, and still-open dollars.

**Price as an experiment:** quote a fixed fee that exceeds fully loaded delivery labor, tools/data, and support with a margin; constrain both case count and hours. The re-research suggested roughly $10,000 fixed plus about 5% of attributable verified recovery as one experiment, but these are unvalidated figures, especially for a smaller solo-founder case cap. Adjust to the customer's conservative, verifiable residual value and procurement reality; do not take a pilot that requires an outcome fee or free founder labor to be contribution-positive. Define attribution and payment timing before any contingent fee. Confirm the prospect can onboard and pay an India-based vendor before treating a verbal yes as a sale. Do not promise a recovery percentage.

The entry product is a **resolution sprint**, not a free audit or a broad replacement pitch. SPS already advertises a [free audit using customer deduction data](https://www.spscommerce.com/products/revenue-recovery/) and automation for routine identification, documentation, disputes, and prevention. That vendor claim establishes a competing offer, not that this residual wedge exists at every supplier.

## Acquisition sequence

1. **Build a small named-account list.** Use public retailer-supplier relationships, customer/partner pages, and AR/deductions role signals to find suppliers likely to fit the ICP. Verify each account's current process and contact before outreach. Record the evidence for retailer exposure, existing deduction ownership, potential sponsor, and why an unresolved queue is plausible. Do not infer an actual residual dollar pool from public revenue or vendor case studies.
2. **Founder-led direct outreach first.** Contact the economic owner or AR leader with a narrow question about the last case that remained unresolved after their existing process. Ask for a short working session, not a platform demo. Use a synthetic or permissioned example case file to show the deliverable; do not make a customer claim without a real result. Work a small, researched account list and follow up manually; volume campaigns would consume solo-founder time without improving qualification.
3. **Qualify on one real case and the full queue.** In discovery, reconstruct one recent exception, ask for the last 12 months' count and dollars in the same residual state, learn why the incumbent stopped, identify evidence owners and retailer route, and ask who can approve a paid read-only sprint. Redacted examples are sufficient for discovery. Request sensitive files only under the agreed data terms.
4. **Propose the bounded paid sprint.** Give the sponsor a one-page SOW with scope, inputs, deliverables, fee, hours cap, approval rules, data handling, success measures, and decision date. Confirm that ordinary ready-to-dispute claims are excluded. Begin only when the fee and evidence/access path are agreed.
5. **Deliver, measure, and ask for the next purchase.** Review case files with the operator as they are produced, log decisions and actual actions, obtain Finance's outcome readback, and present the full starting cohort plus labor cost at the decision meeting. Ask for a second paid cohort or ongoing agreement only where value and repeatability are shown. Continue tracking open financial outcomes under the contracted follow-through terms.

**Secondary channel:** in parallel with a small amount of direct discovery, seek introductions from one or two U.S.-based EDI/ERP advisers or deduction specialists with relevant client trust. Use them for access and credibility, not as an untested reseller program; keep customer contract, evidence permission, and delivery responsibility explicit. Expand the channel only after a repeatable paid result. Direct conversations still provide clearer evidence about the buyer, objections, and willingness to pay.

**First four weeks of founder effort:** prepare the synthetic case file, narrow SOW, data-handling explanation, and qualification worksheet; research a capped list of 20–30 high-fit accounts and a few relevant advisers; use scheduled U.S. overlap blocks for discovery; seek one real unresolved example per serious prospect; and propose a paid sprint only when sponsor, evidence, authority, and value pass the gates below. The account count is a time budget, not a conversion forecast. No bulk outreach or external contact is authorized by this plan.

## Qualification gates

| Gate | Required evidence before advancing |
|---|---|
| Residual problem | Recent cases remain unresolved or denied **after** the current platform/team/BPO acted; age, amount, and stop reason are visible. |
| Materiality | A bounded cohort has conservative recoverable or avoidable value that can support the quoted fee; gross deduction volume alone is insufficient. |
| Controllability | At least some cases appear resolvable through available evidence, coordination, route selection, or timely action rather than only discretionary negotiation. This is a hypothesis until the cases are worked. |
| Access and authority | A Finance/O2C sponsor can provide the export, approved documents, named Logistics/Commercial contacts, a customer-operated retailer channel, and a finance readback. |
| Purchase path | A named budget owner can sign the fixed-fee pilot and agrees to a production/no-go meeting. |
| Remote trust and vendor setup | The buyer accepts an India-based remote operator, can approve the proposed data location/access method, and can onboard/pay the vendor without requiring a U.S. presence or certification the founder does not have. Do not assume these requirements; ask. |
| Delivery economics | The case/hour cap and fee support positive contribution before any success fee. |

If a prospect cannot provide an unresolved cohort or access to evidence, record it as a disqualification or an access blocker. Do not turn a single difficult case into an unbounded custom-services engagement.

## Sales materials needed before outreach

- One-page offer and SOW template with exact boundaries, authority, data terms, and pilot fee logic.
- A synthetic case file showing source references, contradictions, economic decision, action packet, and separate approved-versus-paid amounts.
- A short data-request checklist: case export fields, supporting files, named contacts, retailer route, and periodic finance/remittance readback.
- A qualification worksheet for residual cases, exposure, incumbent stop reason, access, sponsor, and purchase path.
- A result template that reports full-cohort denominator, selected-case rule, action funnel, financial outcomes, and labor-based contribution.
- A plain-language security and access note: where files are held, who sees them, whether model providers process them, retention/deletion, and a customer-operated route for retailer actions. Use only the access method and processing permissions agreed with the customer.

**Example first message:** “I’m working on deductions that remain open after the normal platform or BPO process. Could you walk me through one recent case your team could not close—what blocked it, the dollars involved, and what finally happened? If a bounded queue exists, I can propose a paid, read-only resolution sprint for one retailer. I can send a sample case-file format first.” This is proposed copy; no message has been sent.

## What to measure and when to change course

Track the full funnel: verified ICP accounts contacted; economic-owner conversations; prospects able to show a real residual queue; qualified opportunities; paid SOWs and fees collected; time from first contact to signature; cohort dollars; cases decisioned and acted on; verified recovery and correct closures; repeat purchases; and contribution after fully loaded labor, tools, and support. Record remote-trust, India-vendor onboarding, procurement, and data-access objections as carefully as sales conversion. Measure founder hours spent on sales and case delivery separately.

At each pilot decision meeting, continue only if the fixed-fee work is contribution-positive, the customer acts on the output, and the cases reveal repeatable work that software could reduce. A repeat purchase or paid expansion from an independent customer strengthens the demand signal. If U.S. access/onboarding prevents a paid pilot, if incumbents already solve the relevant cases, if evidence cannot be obtained, if recovery depends mainly on relationship concessions, or if manual effort stays bespoke, narrow or abandon this wedge. Test an India-first adaptation only as a separate market hypothesis with its own customer cases and economics. Initial paid pilots test demand and delivery economics; they do not by themselves establish population-wide incidence or the supplied methodology's P25 exposure gate.
