## Decisions and current thesis

**Latest status (2026-09-29):** The retailer-deduction documents below preserve the earlier design, not a validated build choice. The strict recheck found no seven-gate winner. After the user relaxed the scale and incumbent constraints, a new report provisionally recommended pre-renewal Microsoft NCE commitment assurance for Pax8/HaloPSA MSPs. No MVP code, paying customer, or verified differentiation for that candidate exists in this repository.

The selected **build-worthy hypothesis** is post-incumbent resolution of difficult retailer deductions for suppliers. Existing products already cover much of the routine deduction workflow. The proposed entry is the unresolved or denied cases left after an ERP, deduction platform, or BPO has done its job. The current MVP direction is **software-led and customer-operated**: the product onboards the user, reconstructs the supported case from uploaded evidence, prompts for missing facts, guides an economic decision and customer-approved action, and tracks the finance-verified outcome. Founder analysis is not the normal delivery path. The initial supported exception workflow, including retailer scope and deduction type, has not been selected or validated.

The mature product would maintain an evidence-backed economic case from purchase order and commercial terms through shipment, retailer receipt, invoice, remittance, deduction, dispute, and final cash. It would eventually identify and correct repeat upstream causes. Its definition of done is **economically correct financial finality**, not dispute submission or approval. It should make uncertainty explicit, keep each active case assigned to a next action and deadline, and reserve commercial or authority-bound decisions for people.

The most important unknown remains the size of the **post-incumbent, software-controllable residual pool per customer**. The reports establish a plausible workflow and structural boundaries but do not establish the methodology's required incidence, P25 exposure, controllable fraction, residual ARR capacity, or paid-entry economics. The seven hard gates remain conditional. Scenario numbers and product examples in the reports are planning models, not observed customer results.
